Nenrin is a Japanese-era-year (wareki) reference table. Unlike the many Japanese-only wareki converters on the App Store, Nenrin is built for non-Japanese readers who must read Japanese documents.

What is different:
- Full English interface, while the calendar data itself (era names, zodiac, rokuyo) stays in Japanese with kana and romaji readings, so users learn to read the real documents rather than only see a translation.
- 501 continuous years (1600-2100) on one scrollable list, including Edo-period eras, not only Meiji onward.
- Bidirectional lookup: era year, age and Western year, from any direction. Non-existent era years such as Showa 100 are also converted.
- Cultural context layers: rokuyo (six-day luck cycle), moon phase, kyusei nine-star astrology, yakudoshi unlucky years, gaju longevity celebrations - presented as a reference for understanding Japanese customs.
- Tapping any Japanese term in the app (era name, stem-branch, rokuyo, nine-star sign, yakudoshi, gaju) opens a written explanation of its meaning, reading and cultural background, in the user's language. This is the core of the app's educational intent and is not offered by the existing wareki converters.
- Per-year notes (up to 400 characters), for genealogy and family-register research.

The app is intended as an educational and cultural reference for Japanese-language learners, genealogy researchers, and foreign residents in Japan filling in official forms.
